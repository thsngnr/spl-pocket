Connecting to Splunk

SPL Pocket connects directly to the Splunk management port (8089 by default). If your servers are on a private network, connect to your VPN first. Splunk's default self-signed certificate is supported: the app shows the certificate fingerprint and asks you to approve it once.

Recommended role

Create a dedicated role and use a token for its user. Tested capabilities: search, list_health, license_read, list_deployment_server, list_indexer_cluster, list_settings, rest_apps_view, rest_properties_get; searchable indexes *, _internal, _audit, _introspection, _ds*. No edit or delete capabilities are needed. With less access the app tells you where data can't be read.

Notifications

Turn on Settings › Notifications in the app and choose your topics. iOS decides when background checks run (usually a few times a day); your VPN must be connected at that moment. iOS skips checks in Low Power Mode or after you swipe the app away.

Subscriptions

Manage or cancel your subscription in the iOS Settings app › your name › Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new device, open the Pro screen in SPL Pocket and tap Restore Purchases.
