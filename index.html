SPL Pocket

An iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch app for Splunk Enterprise administrators.

SPL Pocket connects directly from your device to your own Splunk Enterprise servers (management port 8089, usually over your VPN) and shows cluster health, license usage, security advisories that affect your version, silent data sources, pipeline queues, certificates and more. There is no account, no cloud service and no relay server.
